Research PublicationDecember 26, 2025

Metabolic monitoring among antipsychotic users with diabetes in US in Psychiatry Research

Using a large EHR cohort of newly diagnosed T2D from Epic Cosmos (n = 469,503), we examined monitoring and control of HbA1c (≤8%), blood pressure (≤140/90 mmHg) and LDL cholesterol (≤100mg/dL) in the three years after cohort entry (i.e., index prescription). Index prescription was categorized as second generation antipsychotics (SGA), those not prescribed SGA but prescribed first generation antipsychotics (FGA), and those prescribed neither in the two years after T2D diagnosis.
